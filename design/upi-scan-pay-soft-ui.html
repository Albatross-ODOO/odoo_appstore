UPI Scan & Pay

A UPI payment QR on every customer invoice and quotation PDF. The buyer scans it, the amount and the payee are already filled in, they just enter their UPI PIN. Works for any company in any currency — a non-INR amount is converted to INR automatically. No Indian localisation module required.

How it looks

One card, printed on both documents

The card is a single shared QWeb template. On the invoice it sits below the totals and payment terms, where Odoo already puts its own payment QR. On the quotation it sits directly under the totals. Colours are neutral so it fits any company's document layout; the card inherits the document font.

Card anatomy

What is on the card, and why

Same template, zoomed. Every element answers a question the buyer has at the moment of paying.

  1. QR on a raised tile

    A real upi://pay QR (the one above scans). Rendered server-side with Odoo's own barcode helper at error-correction level M, embedded as a data URI, so nothing is fetched while printing. 30 mm on paper — scannable from a phone held at arm's length.

  2. The amount, large

    The number the buyer will see in their app: the grand total of the invoice or order (a setting can switch invoices to amount due). Documents in another currency are converted to INR with Odoo's exchange rate, and the original amount and rate are printed under it so the buyer understands the figure.

  3. UPI ID · Payee · Reference

    The UPI ID in monospace so it can be typed if the QR is smudged. The reference is the document number and is also passed to the app as the transaction reference and note, so it appears on the payer's statement.

  4. Caption & app chips

    The caption is editable in Settings (translatable). The chips are plain text — no third-party logos shipped in the module.

UPI ID format OK
Rules the settings enforceserver-side, on Save
UPI ID

Must look like handle@bank: ^[A-Za-z0-9._-]{2,}@[A-Za-z]{2,}$. Saved lower-case. Empty = feature off for that company.

Payee name

Defaults to the company name. Letters, digits and spaces only (UPI apps drop or reject other characters). Max 99 characters.

Caption

Translatable text under the QR. Default: "Scan with any UPI app — the amount and payee are pre-filled, just confirm with your UPI PIN."

Amount on QR

Grand total (default) or Amount due — applies to invoices; orders always use the total.

Currency

Any. A non-INR document is converted with Odoo's rate for INR at the document date. If no INR rate exists, Settings shows a warning and the card is skipped rather than printing a wrong number.

Switches

Invoices on, quotations/orders on, replace built-in QR off. All company-dependent — multi-company safe.

Preview

Computed on the settings record from the two fields above — encodes ₹ 1.00 with note "Verification" so a scan is harmless.

No new menu, no new model, no access rights to assign. Install → type the UPI ID → Save → print.

  1. Scan the card on the PDF

    From the printed page or from the PDF on a screen. The QR contains the full intent; no internet round-trip to Odoo is needed.

  2. Payee and amount are already there

    The app shows the company as payee and the exact amount, locked. The note and reference carry the document number.

  3. Buyer enters their own UPI PIN

    The 4- or 6-digit PIN the buyer created with their bank when they linked the account in their UPI app. It belongs to the buyer; Odoo never sets, sees or stores it. The money lands in the company's UPI-linked bank account within seconds.

  4. Accountant reconciles as usual

    The document number is on the bank statement line (note / reference). Registering the payment stays Odoo-standard — the module does not touch payment records.

QR payload

The upi://pay intent, exactly as encoded

NPCI's deep-link format. Six parameters, percent-encoded. This is what the invoice QR above contains.

upi://pay?pa=albatross%40okhdfcbank&pn=Albatross%20Pvt%20Ltd&am=12980.00&cu=INR&tn=Payment%20for%20INV%2F2026%2F00042&tr=INV%2F2026%2F00042
ParamMeaningSource in OdooRule
paPayee address (VPA)company.upi_qr_idRequired. Validated on save.
pnPayee name shown in the appcompany.upi_qr_payee_name → company nameSanitised to letters / digits / spaces.
amAmount, pre-filled and lockedGrand total (invoices can be switched to amount due)Two decimals, dot separator, must be > 0. Converted to INR first if the document is in another currency.
cuCurrencyAlways INRUPI apps accept INR only, so a USD / EUR / AED document is converted with currency._convert() at the document date and the original amount + rate are printed on the card.
tnTransaction note"Payment for " + document numberAppears on both parties' statements. Max 80 chars.
trTransaction referenceInvoice: payment reference or number · Order: numberLets the accountant match the bank line to the document.

QR rendering

env['ir.actions.report'].barcode('QR', uri, width=300, height=300, barLevel='M') → PNG bytes → base64. This is the helper Odoo itself uses for EPC and Swiss QR bills; it runs on reportlab, already a core dependency, so nothing to install on the customer's server.

Currency conversion

In the mixin: doc.currency_id._convert(amount, INR, doc.company_id, doc_date) — the same call Odoo uses for its own reports, identical in 18 and 19. INR documents pass through untouched. For a non-INR document the card prints the INR figure large and the original amount + rate in a line under it. Rates come from Accounting → Configuration → Currencies; on Enterprise the automatic rate updater keeps them fresh, on Community they are entered by hand. If INR has no rate at all, the card is skipped and the settings page says why — never a silently wrong amount.

Why a mixin, not copy-paste

The reference module duplicated the compute in two models. One abstract model holds the URI rules, encoding and rendering; each document only answers "which amount, which reference, when". Adding a third document later is ten lines.

4

When the card prints

The template checks one computed field; the rules live in Python where they can be tested.

Prints

Customer invoice or receipt that is not fully paid, company switch on, UPI ID set — any currency. Quotation or confirmed order with a total, switch on — any currency.

Never prints

Credit notes, vendor bills, cancelled documents, fully paid invoices, zero-amount documents, companies with no UPI ID, non-INR documents when no INR exchange rate exists.

Multi-company

Everything reads doc.company_id, never the current user's company, so a batch print across companies is correct.

Indian layout

l10n_in renders invoices through a primary copy of the invoice template. Primary copies receive every extension of their parent, so the card appears on the Indian layout too — verified against the 18 and 19 source.

Studio reports

Not covered, as with any inherited report — noted on the store page.

7

Odoo's own QR

Avoiding two codes on one invoice.

Odoo's "QR Codes" setting (Invoicing → Customer Payments) prints a bank QR in div#qrcode: EPC in Europe, Swiss QR-bill, and — when l10n_in is installed and the company's UPI Id is filled on the company form — an Indian UPI QR. A customer who already uses that would get two UPI codes.

Proposed: a third switch, Replace Odoo's built-in payment QR, off by default. When on and our card prints, the module rewrites the show_qr expression so the native block is skipped. Both versions expose the same t-set, so one xpath covers both. With the switch off, nothing of Odoo's is touched. The store page explains this in one line.

Decisions

Recorded on 22 September 2026

Answers to the Phase 1 questions. One item is still open: the store name.

Technical name

Folder renamed UPI_QRcode → upi_qr_payment in both the 18 and the 19 repo.

Done
Amount on the QR: grand total

The full amount of the invoice or order is encoded, not the balance. A per-company setting "Amount on QR" (Grand total / Amount due) is kept for customers who part-pay; default is Grand total. Fully paid invoices still do not print the card.

Decidedtotal
Any currency, converted automatically

The module is global: a company running in USD, EUR, AED or any other currency gets the same card; the amount is converted to INR with Odoo's exchange rate at the document date, and the original amount and rate are printed under the INR figure. If no INR rate is available the card is skipped and Settings explains what to add.

Decidedconvert, never skip silently
Odoo's built-in Indian UPI QR

A "Replace Odoo's built-in payment QR" switch, off by default.

Decidedswitch, default off
Quotations and orders

Both, behind one switch.

Decidedyes
Scope of v1.0 and card position

PDFs only; the card sits full-width below the totals. Portal card, form tab and e-mail QR stay on the list for a later release.

Decidedyes
Store name — open

People find modules on apps.odoo.com by searching a need: "UPI", "QR", "payment", "invoice". The name should carry those words; the summary carries the benefit and the app names. Shortlist:

  • UPI QR Payment on Invoice & Quotation — keyword-complete, 37 characters, reads well in search results.
  • Invoice & Quotation UPI QR Code — Scan & Pay — document-first, for buyers searching "invoice QR".
  • UPI Scan & Pay — short and brandable, but the store search needs the summary to carry the keywords.
  • Dynamic UPI QR — Invoice & Quotation PDF — "dynamic" signals that the amount is pre-filled, which is the differentiator over static QR modules.
RecommendedUPI QR Payment on Invoice & Quotation, with summary "Scan & Pay: dynamic UPI QR with the amount pre-filled on invoice and quotation PDFs — any UPI app, any currency".
