How it works

Open the app. Pick a conversation. Read.

Chat Monitor is its own app in the Odoo menu, visible only to users in the Chat Monitor Manager group. It reads the messages Odoo already stores — it does not copy, forward or change them.

1

Browse the list

Every conversation, newest activity first, with participant avatars, the time of the last message, a one-line preview and a type badge.

2

Narrow it down

All Types / Direct / Group / Channels, then Today, Yesterday, 7 Days or All — and search by conversation title, participant or visitor name.

3

Read the transcript

The whole conversation on the right: authors, avatars, local times, formatted messages and the files that were shared.

The conversation list

Every conversation, including the ones you are not in

Direct messages between two colleagues, private groups, public and restricted channels — all listed, whether or not the manager is a member.

Threads under their channel — a thread started from a message is titled like in Discuss: Sales › Q4 pricing.
Live Chat and WhatsApp too — visitor chats (Live Chat app) and WhatsApp conversations (Enterprise) appear under Channels, each with its own badge.
Website visitors by name — guests are listed as participants with their own avatar, and you can search for them.
Archived channels stay visible — marked with an Archived badge, so hiding a channel never hides its history.
Date filters that mean what they say — Today, Yesterday and 7 Days (the last seven calendar days, today included) in the manager's timezone. The filter trims the transcript as well.

The transcript

Read it as it was written

Messages keep their formatting and links. Each one shows its author, avatar and time; the header lists the participants.

Files, one click away — attachments are listed under their message and download through a route that checks the monitor group first.
Long histories, fast — a conversation opens on its latest 300 messages; Load older messages brings in the next 300.
Every author named — website visitors appear with their own name and avatar, and a message deleted in Discuss reads This message has been removed.
Big groups stay tidy — the header shows the first eight participants and a +N more tag with everyone else in its tooltip.

Anywhere you work

On a phone, and in dark mode

On a phone the list sits above the open conversation and the header folds into one line. On Enterprise, the console follows Odoo's dark mode.

Access & privacy

Read-only, and only for the monitor group

Installing the app adds one group, Chat Monitor Manager. Members can read every Discuss conversation on the database, including private direct chats — that is the purpose of the app, and a responsibility: make sure monitoring is covered by your workplace policy and, where required, that staff have been informed.

Checked on the server — the console's data calls refuse anyone outside the group, whether the call comes from the browser, a portal account or an API key.
Look, don't touch — monitors get no ordinary access to other people's conversations, so they cannot post, join or edit through Discuss.
A clear no for everyone else — the menu is hidden from other users, and anyone who opens the console link gets a You do not have access screen.

Everything included

Built for oversight, not for chatting

Every conversation type

Direct chats, groups, channels and their threads, Live Chat and WhatsApp conversations — each with a clear badge.

Search as you type

Matches conversation titles, participants and visitors. It waits for a short pause in typing, and a slow answer never replaces a newer one.

Times you recognise

Shown in the manager's timezone with the date and time format of their language, in the style of Discuss: “Today at 03:11 PM”, “Yesterday at 05:00 PM”, or the full date.

Fast on big databases

The list is built with a few grouped database queries, and long conversations load 300 messages at a time.

Ready to translate

Every label and message of the console is translatable, and the translation template ships with the app.

Refresh on demand

One button reloads the conversation list, another reloads the open conversation; the view scrolls to the latest message automatically.

Compatibility

No third-party services, no subscriptions, no settings to fill in. The app uses only Odoo's own calls between your browser and your server.

What the console will never do

Oversight without side effects

No replies, no edits

The console has no message box and no edit or delete buttons. To take part in a conversation, use Discuss as usual.

No silent membership

Reading a conversation does not add the monitor to it, and nothing changes for the people chatting.

No data leaving your server

Everything stays in your Odoo database; no third-party service is ever contacted.

FAQ

Before you install

Do users know they are being monitored?

The app shows no indicator inside Discuss. Informing staff is your organisation's responsibility under local employment and privacy law.

Does it show messages posted on records (chatter)?

No. It covers Discuss conversations only: direct chats, groups, channels and their threads, plus Live Chat and WhatsApp conversations. Chatter on invoices, tasks and other records is out of scope.

Where do Live Chat and WhatsApp conversations appear?

Under All Types and Channels, each with its own badge. Website visitors are shown by the name they gave, with their own avatar.

What do users outside the group see?

Nothing changes for them: the Chat Monitor menu is hidden, and opening the console link shows a You do not have access screen instead of any data.

Does it work with many conversations?

Yes. The list is built with a few grouped database queries, and a conversation opens on its latest 300 messages with a button to load older ones. Date filters keep long-running channels manageable.

Can I use it in my language?

Yes. All texts are translatable (a translation template is included), and dates and times follow the format of each user's language in Settings › Languages.

Is anything sent outside my server?

Nothing. The app uses only Odoo's own calls between your browser and your Odoo instance.

Multi-company?

The console lists all Discuss conversations on the database regardless of company. Restrict the manager group accordingly if you need per-company monitors.
