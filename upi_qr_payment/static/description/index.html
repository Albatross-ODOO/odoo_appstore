Invoices and quotations

Printed on both documents. Any currency.

Customer invoices and receipts carry the card while an amount is outstanding. Quotations and confirmed orders carry it so the customer can pay an advance straight from the quote. What the QR contains

The standard UPI intent, nothing proprietary

upi://pay?pa=yourid@bank&pn=Your Company&am=12980.00&cu=INR&tn=Payment for INV/2026/0001&tr=INV/2026/0001
pa / pn — your UPI ID and payee name from Settings
am / cu — the amount in INR, locked in the app
tn / tr — note and reference with the document number, for reconciliation

FAQ

Before you install

Do I need a payment gateway or a merchant account?

No. The QR points at your existing UPI ID. The money goes bank-to-bank, with no fees and no intermediary. Odoo never touches the payment.

Where does the UPI PIN come from?

It is the buyer's own PIN, created with their bank when they linked their account in their UPI app. Nothing PIN-related is stored or configured in Odoo.

Do I need the Indian localisation?

No. The module depends on Sales and Invoicing only. If l10n_in is installed the card also prints on the Indian invoice layout, and a switch lets you hide Odoo's built-in UPI QR so the customer sees one code.

My company runs in USD. Does it still work?

Yes. UPI apps only accept INR, so the amount is converted with your INR exchange rate at the document date and the original amount and rate are printed on the card. Keep the INR rate current under Accounting → Configuration → Currencies.

What about part-paid invoices?

The QR encodes the grand total by default. Switch QR Amount to Amount Due and it encodes the outstanding balance instead. Fully paid invoices never print the card.

Does the payment get registered in Odoo automatically?

No — UPI has no callback into Odoo. The invoice number travels in the note and reference, so the bank statement line matches the invoice at a glance and reconciliation stays standard Odoo.
