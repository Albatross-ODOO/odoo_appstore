Check-Out Client Visit

STEP 1 / 8
  1. Client requiredLive server search of Contacts by name, phone or email as you type; pick one to link it (phone & email prefill) or "Create …" to make a new one.
  2. Contact person optionalWho the rep actually met. Skippable.
  3. Phone requiredDrives duplicate detection on the server.
  4. Email optionalValidated only if entered.
  5. Odoo recordsCreate contact / Create lead toggles. If a contact was linked in step 1 the contact toggle is hidden. Whatever is created is assigned to the rep as salesperson and tagged "Field Lead".
  6. Visit notes optionalFree text, becomes the lead description.
  7. Visit photo requiredInline camera (rear by default, tap to flip) with capture / retake — or the phone's native camera app as a fallback. Photos are downscaled to 1280px before upload.
  8. Review & check outSummary of everything; the green button completes the visit and logs the check-out GPS point.
Next only when validThe primary button stays pressed-in (disabled) until a required field has a value. Enter advances too.
Skip reads honestlyOptional steps label the button "Skip" when empty and "Next" once something is typed.
Edit without losing placeTapping a folded row re-expands it; later rows stay in place, so nothing is re-typed.
Progress is literalThe bar is the step count, not an estimate — 8 steps, 12.5% each.
Phone-firstOn phones the form is a bottom sheet with a drag handle; on desktop a centred card. Closing it keeps your draft until the visit is saved.
